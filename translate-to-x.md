TYPICAL PHRASES

POSTS AND REPLIES

- In a reply, talk directly to the person and answer their specific point. Don't recap or repeat the original post. Leave room for the conversation to continue.

- Do not automatically turn the final sentence into a punchline. In a serious post, a short joke at the end is fine only if the speaker made it.
- Do not split the text into a thread unless the speaker requests one.

EDITING THE TRANSCRIPT

CONTENT PRESERVATION
